Graph ML

Graph learning for anti-money laundering.

We compared five graph-learning approaches on AMLworld under one protocol. A graph transformer with flow-conservation attention and temporal features gave the best illicit-class F1 (77.6%).

Team: Avin Safari Shirzi, Mohamed Abouelella, Rizwan Haidar and Haofan Wen

Polarization detection across English and Urdu.

NLP

XAIHate: explainable hate-speech detection for Urdu.

We built a word-level explanation method for Urdu and Roman Urdu where LIME failed on Urdu script, served through a Flask web app. DistilBERT reached 87.7% accuracy on Urdu.
