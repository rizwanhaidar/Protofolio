Graph learning for anti-money laundering.

Can reconstructing transaction features improve detection of rare illicit transfers? A reproduced graph-learning baseline was tested against an edge-feature variant.

Completed master’s lab · Collaborative study

My role: Reproduced Multi-GIN + EU and implemented the partial FRAD-B edge-feature variant; contributed the introduction, theory, experimental setup and results sections.

Finding / scope: On synthetic AMLworld Small-HI, illicit-class F1 was 59.9 ± 7.1 for the baseline and 54.07 ± 10.7 for FRAD-B. No improvement was established.

SELECTED STUDY 03
University of Bonn
Master’s project · University of Bonn, CAISA Lab · Team 25

NLP

Polarization detection across English and Urdu.

How well can models identify polarization in English and Urdu when the languages and label frequencies differ? The team compared trained classifiers with prompted language models.

Completed master’s project · Team 25 · Report: 9 February 2026

My role: Team member with Maaz Asad, Maria Yasir and Aqib Amir Khan. Individual implementation ownership is not specified in the report.

Finding / scope: Monolingual RemBERT achieved macro-F1 of 0.7630 on Urdu and 0.4615 on English. Language and label distributions differ, so these are not directly comparable language benchmarks.
