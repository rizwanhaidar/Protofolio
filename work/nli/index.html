NLP · CASE STUDY

University of Bonn

Natural language inference beyond benchmark accuracy.

Can a model trained to infer relationships between sentences generalize to unfamiliar genres? We compared three architectures on SNLI and MultiNLI.

Team project · Mining Media Data II · with Maaz Asad and Maria Yasir

91.04% → 74.31% BERT accuracy

IN THIS PROJECT

QuestionApproachResultNext steps
01

Question

How much does large-scale pretraining contribute to natural language inference, and do high benchmark scores survive domain shift and shortcut-focused diagnostics?

02

Approach

We compared a three-layer, six-head Transformer initialized with 300-dimensional GloVe vectors against BERT-base-uncased and DeBERTa-v3-base. After cleaning SNLI, we used 549,361 training, 9,842 validation and 9,824 test pairs, and evaluated held-out accuracy, per-class recall, MultiNLI transfer and diagnostic subsets for overlap, negation and length mismatch.

03

Result

SNLI test accuracy was 91.04% for BERT, 90.30% for DeBERTa and 80.84% for the custom Transformer. On combined MultiNLI validation data, accuracy fell to 74.31%, 70.83% and 44.91%. High in-domain accuracy did not carry across genres.

04

Next steps

Evaluation on HANS or SNLI-hard, MultiNLI fine-tuning and adversarial augmentation would test generalization further.
