Polarization detection across English and Urdu.

How well can models identify polarization in English and Urdu when language and label frequencies differ? We compared fine-tuned encoders with prompted LLMs.

Team project · Introduction to NLP, CAISA Lab · with Maaz Asad, Maria Yasir and Aqib Amir Khan

IN THIS PROJECT

QuestionApproachResultNext steps
01

Question

How do fine-tuned multilingual transformers and prompted LLMs compare for multilabel polarization classification when English and Urdu differ strongly in language and label distribution?

02

Approach

Using the POLAR dataset (SemEval-2026 Task 9, Subtask 2), we classified posts into five polarization categories. We fine-tuned RemBERT and XLM-RoBERTa in monolingual, joint and cross-lingual settings with per-label threshold tuning, and prompted GPT-OSS, Llama 3.1 and Qwen3 with six zero- and few-shot templates. Macro-F1 was the primary metric.

03

Result

Monolingual RemBERT performed best, with macro-F1 of 0.763 on Urdu and 0.462 on English. Fine-tuned models outperformed prompting in both languages, and cross-lingual zero-shot transfer fell to 0.20–0.30.

04

Next steps

English data augmentation, retrieval-based few-shot selection and lightweight adapters for cross-lingual transfer.
