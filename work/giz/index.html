01

Program context

GET.transform is a European multi-donor programme supporting public-sector partners in power-sector transformation. The internship connected international development and energy-sector work with shared project-management infrastructure.

02

Verified contribution

Migrated project-management data from Excel to Microsoft Lists. Helped structure SharePoint and Microsoft Teams connections, maintained SharePoint pages and developed process flows to inform system requirements. Onboarded colleagues through virtual and in-person training and produced a user manual.

03

Approach & outcome

The completed internship delivered a project-data migration, colleague onboarding and process documentation. The reference also confirms project status updates and collaboration with an international team. No numerical efficiency gain is claimed.

04

Earlier CV context

The earlier CV additionally describes Airtable migration, role-based access controls and Power BI KPI reporting. These are CV-reported activities and are not independently corroborated by the employer reference.

05

Technical perspective

Reporting infrastructure must account for both the structure of program data and the people who maintain and consume it.
