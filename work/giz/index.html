IN THIS CASE STUDY

ContextMy workResult
01

Context

GET.transform is a European multi-donor programme, implemented by GIZ, that helps public-sector partners transform their power sectors. Its team works across Belgium, Chile, Central America, Germany, Eswatini, Lesotho, Mozambique, Peru and Uganda.

02

My work

I migrated project-management data from Excel to Microsoft Lists and assessed process flows to define requirements for a new project-management system. I built folder structures, landing pages and synchronized SharePoint–Teams connections, and maintained the project’s SharePoint pages. I trained colleagues through virtual and in-person sessions, wrote the user manual, and presented project status overviews in team meetings.

03

Result

One shared project-data environment that the team adopted.
