IN THIS CASE STUDY

ContextMy workResult
01

Context

Toyota Financial Services reports CO2 and portfolio figures per market to its governance, risk and compliance (GRC) managers every month and quarter, and its risk reporting draws on macroeconomic factors. These outputs feed audit-critical reporting, so every figure has to trace back to source.

02

My work

I automated the monthly and quarterly reporting extracts for 9 markets, replacing manual preparation, and integrated macroeconomic factors into the risk reporting data. I built reconciliation checks that compare counts and balances between legacy sources and the Snowflake warehouse, and migrated legacy data into Snowflake through 5+ ETL pipelines. I also built a cost-analysis dashboard that shows what the team’s data tools cost and where usage concentrates.

03

Result

Three workstreams running on the warehouse: automated GRC extracts for 9 markets on a monthly and quarterly cadence, macroeconomic factors in risk reporting, and one view of the team’s tool costs.
