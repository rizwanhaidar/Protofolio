FAST NUCES

XAIHate: explainable hate-speech detection for Urdu.

Hate-speech classification for Urdu, Roman Urdu and English, with word-level explanations that a non-expert can read.

Final Year Project · team of three, supervised by Umair Arshad and Dr. Omer Beg

87.7% Urdu accuracy
TF-IDFNaive BayesRandom ForestBERTDistilBERTLIMEFlask

IN THIS PROJECT

QuestionApproachResultNext steps
01

Question

How can hate-speech detection in Urdu, Roman Urdu and English be paired with explanations that show why a text received its label?

02

Approach

We compared TF-IDF models (Naive Bayes, logistic regression, random forest) with BERT, DistilBERT and RoBERTa. Because LIME did not handle Urdu script, we built a word-removal explainer that measures each word’s contribution and validated it against LIME. A Flask web app showed highlighted text, contribution charts and Twitter word clouds.

03

Result

DistilBERT reached 87.7% accuracy on Urdu and 88.05% on Roman Urdu; BERT reached 96.11% on English. On Urdu, 81.9% of our explainer’s top-ranked words matched LIME’s.

04

Next steps

Extending the explainer to deep-learning models and growing the Urdu and Roman Urdu datasets.
